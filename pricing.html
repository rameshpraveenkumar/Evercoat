Cost Breakdown Factors

What Determines Your Final Quote?

Unlike informal contractors who offer arbitrary estimates over the phone, EVERCOAT prices work scientifically based on four measurable parameters:

Wall Condition & Substrate Prep

Surfaces with deep dampness, efflorescence, or peeling paint require chemical neutralization and skimming coats.

Paint Grade & Sheen Selection

Premium washable emulsions, low-VOC antibacterial paints, and elastomeric weather barriers vary by formulation cost.

Ceiling Heights & Architectural Detailing

Double-height stairwells, decorative cornices, and extensive masking require specialized scaffolding equipment.
