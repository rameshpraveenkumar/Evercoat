Restoration Methodology

The Anatomy of a Flawless Finish

Long-lasting paint finishes are engineered from the wall outwards. Here is our systematic four-stage protocol applied on every residential project.

01
Inspection

Substrate Moisture Testing

We test deep substrate equilibrium with pinless RF moisture meters. Primer is never applied until walls read under 12% moisture.

Prevents blistering & efflorescence
02
Preparation

99% Dustless HEPA Sanding

Planetary vacuum sanders with 0.3-micron filtration eliminate airborne drywall dust, preserving clean air and immaculate furnishings.

Clean air & spotless floors
03
Priming

Anchor Primer Sealer

Full-penetration stain-blocking primer creates a chemical bridge between cured plaster and topcoat, ensuring 100% adhesion.

Zero peel or patch telegraphing
04
Finishing

Dual Architectural Coats

Two cross-rolled coats of premium low-VOC emulsion applied with laser-straight cut-ins along all moldings and ceiling transitions.

Uniform sheen & full washability
