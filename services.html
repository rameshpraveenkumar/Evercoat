Our Services

Professional residential painting and whole-home renovation services. Thoughtfully delivered improvement solutions built with care, quality and dustless preparation for every Austin home.

Service 01

Interior Painting

Our interior painting service is designed around clean lines, uniform sheen, and zero mess. We protect your floors with heavy drop cloths, repair wall cracks and nail holes, sand with dustless vacuums, and apply two coats of premium washable, low-VOC acrylic emulsion.

Areas Covered

Living Room Painting
Bedroom Painting
Ceiling & Cornices
Kitchen & Bathroom Walls
Wall Preparation & Skimming
Full Repainting & Color Updates

Core Benefits:

Odour-free application (safe for kids & pets), ultra-washable stain defense, and multi-stage moisture-tested surface prep.

Service 02

Exterior Painting

Protect your residential investment against harsh Texas sun, intense weather shifts, and seasonal moisture. We utilize multi-tiered scaffolding, pressure washing, structural crack filling with fiber mesh, and elastomeric weather-shield barriers.

Key Features & Protection

Weather-Resistant Finishes
High-Pressure Surface Washing
Villa & Bungalow Facades
Boundary Wall Painting
Anti-Fungal & Anti-Algae Seal
Balcony & Parapet Waterproofing

Core Benefits:

Superior thermal reflection keeps interior rooms cooler; advanced cross-linking polymers prevent efflorescence and water seepage.

Texture Finishes

Specialty Treatments

Authentic Venetian Plaster
Raw Microcement Walls
Feature Accent Walls
Tactile Travertine Texture
Metallic & Stucco Glazes
Custom Designer Wall Effects

Minor Renovations

A beautiful paint job requires structurally sound surfaces. Our minor renovation team handles substrate stabilization, moisture barrier injections, drywall patching, baseboard replacement, and architectural trim restoration before any primer is applied.

Refurbishment Scope

Wall Crack Repairs & Skimming
Plastering & Drywall Restoration
Wooden Door & Frame Polish
Baseboard & Trim Installation
Water Damaged Surface Rehab
Small Room Modernization

Core Benefits:

Single-contractor convenience: you don't need to juggle separate carpenters, masons, and painters.
