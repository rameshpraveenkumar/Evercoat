Structured Execution

Our Four-Step Process

From the initial shade consultation to the final white-glove walk-through, every step is predictable and stress-free.

01

Consultation

We discuss your aesthetic preferences, lighting dynamics, functional needs, and recommend sample swatches.

02

Site Inspection

Laser area measurement, wall moisture check, crack assessment, and delivery of a clear itemized quote.

03

Project Execution

Floor masking, dustless sanding, primer coats, two coats of premium emulsion, and continuous site cleanups.

04

Final Inspection

Room-by-room client walk-through, touch-up verification, unmasking, deep cleaning, and client sign-off.
