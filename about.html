A Little About EVERCOAT

EVERCOAT is a painting and home improvement brand focused on helping homeowners create spaces they are proud to live in.

We bring together thoughtful design, careful preparation, quality materials and professional workmanship to deliver home improvement experiences built around each customer's needs.

Whether it is a fresh coat of paint, a new wall finish or a small renovation, our approach is centred on understanding the space and delivering work with attention to detail.
